# Contributing

This is an exploratory discussion draft. Open an issue or discussion with a concrete gap, a correction, an existing mechanism we missed, or an independent example. Small pull requests are welcome.

Keep examples fictional and avoid customer data, credentials, internal infrastructure details or private source links. Do not submit a deployable credential or run an example against a live service as part of review.

Separate advisory explanations from enforceable semantics. Name native specification versions and actual tool support. Mark experimental annotations as non-normative; do not imply a runtime enforces them.

Prefer a profile, mapping or documentation improvement over a new language/runtime. Record validation limitations. A schema check is not execution or proof of authorization.

Original contributions are under the repository's MIT License. Identify copied third-party material and its license; link rather than copy long passages. Disclose material AI assistance. No standards body, employer or community is implied to endorse a contribution.
